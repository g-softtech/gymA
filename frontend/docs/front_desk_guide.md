# 🚀 CortexFit: Front Desk Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to the team! This guide covers everything you need to know to run the front desk smoothly using CortexFit.

---

## 1️⃣ Getting Started
* **Login URL:** [Your Gym's Custom CortexFit URL]
* **Your Account:** You will receive a Magic Link in your email. No passwords needed! Just click the link to sign in.
* **Dashboard Overview:** The main screen shows today's attendance, upcoming classes, and recent check-ins. Keep this tab open all day.

---

## 2️⃣ Checking In Members

### 📱 Fast Check-in (QR Code)
The easiest way for members to check in.
1. Have the member open their CortexFit app or member portal on their phone.
2. Tell them to tap the **"Check In"** button to display their unique QR code.
3. Use the front desk iPad or barcode scanner to scan the code.
4. The screen will flash **<span style="color:green">GREEN (Access Granted)</span>** or **<span style="color:red">RED (Access Denied)</span>**.

### 🔍 Manual Check-in (Name Search)
If a member forgot their phone:
1. Go to the **Check-in** tab on your dashboard.
2. Type their name or email into the search bar.
3. Verify their photo.
4. Click **"Check In"**.

---

## 3️⃣ Handling Payments & Access Issues

If the screen flashes **<span style="color:red">RED (Access Denied)</span>**, read the error message on your screen:

| Error Message | What It Means | What You Should Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Past Due"** | Their last payment failed. | Ask them to open their member app and tap "Update Payment Method". |
| **"No Active Plan"** | Their membership expired. | Direct them to the "Buy Membership" page on their portal. |
| **"Wrong Class"** | They aren't booked for this class. | Check the schedule. If there is space, you can manually add them. |

> [!WARNING]
> **Important Rule:** Do not accept cash for memberships unless specifically authorized by your manager. CortexFit automatically handles all billing online.

---

## 4️⃣ Welcoming New Walk-ins

When a brand new customer walks in to sign up:
1. **Do not create an account for them manually.**
2. Point them to the **QR Code display on the front desk**.
3. Tell them: *"Scan this code with your phone camera! It will take you to our signup page where you can pick a plan and pay in under 2 minutes."*
4. Once they complete checkout on their phone, their account is instantly active and they can enter the gym!
